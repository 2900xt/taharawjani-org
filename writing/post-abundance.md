# Abundance

When we talk about the future after RSI and superintelligent AI, there's this concept of eternal abundance.

Do we think that this can even happen? With the government being incredibly slow, with people resisting, with society not willing to take the leap of faith into this future?

The level of intelligence in Silicon Valley, at frontier labs, is unparalleled anywhere else in the world. The biggest problem is now adoption. How can we use this intelligence to create a society where every human is better off, how can we align it, how can we make sure we're not extinct to a bioweapon in 2 years? And even if there is abundance, will we really benefit? What will the meaning of life be?

There's genuinely such a large economic inefficiency in the adoption of AGI right now. And this might be the last opportunity one has to exploit these inefficiencies before class structures become rigid? So many enterprises and fields still have humans in the loop (law, medicine, finance, even engineering), and all of their intellectual labor is worthless now. Yet we're still chasing education and degrees like they have ANY economic value beyond just a fancy paper. Wake up. That world is gone, stop coping before it's too late.

# Speaking of,

Will social structure become fixed post-abundance? Will there be mobility? Perfect equality? I don't understand how UBI is EVER going to be implemented - like the government will have to, obviously, but how? We're in so much debt right now.

# At the end of the day

It's night. Nobody f*cking knows what's gonna happen. It's an exciting time to be alive, yes. But, I fear that we as a society aren't really getting ready for the insane intelligence explosion that is about to happen this year. I guess we're just gonna have to go with the flow.

So many questions. So little answers. Such *indefinite optimism*.
